    Under the hood

    From a selfie to a finished look in under 10 seconds

    This demo simulates the pipeline. In production each step is a real model call, and the whole chain runs on GPU infrastructure behind our API.

    01

    Capture

    Customer uploads a photo or snaps one in-store at a kiosk. Face and body are detected automatically.

    02

    Understand the body

    Pose estimation and human parsing map shoulders, torso and arms, and mask out the garment they're wearing.

    03

    Dress the body

    A diffusion try-on model warps the catalog garment onto the body, preserving prints, folds and fabric texture.

    04

    Deliver

    Result is cached per photo + garment, so swapping shirts is instant. Looks can be saved, shared and bought.
